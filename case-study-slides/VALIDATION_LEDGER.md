## Metric definitions to request

- Team members: unique enabled users, monthly active users, sustained users, and role mix.
- Programs: funded programs in scope during the stated reporting period.
- Teams: persistent delivery teams, not temporary training cohorts.
- Productivity: cycle time, throughput, accepted change volume, escaped defects, rework, or cost per verified outcome. State the baseline and comparison cohort.
- Token volume: input, output, cached, and reasoning tokens by model and period. Tie consumption to cost and verified delivery outcomes.
